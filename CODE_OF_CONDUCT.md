# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CryptoDesk AI contributors, maintainers, and participants are committed to a
welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of background, identity, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible security disclosure.
- Use project spaces for collaboration relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other conduct that
would reasonably make participation unsafe are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method published on the
repository owner's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, request
private contact without posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical. This policy promises no specific
response time or outcome.

## Basis

This is a concise project-specific conduct policy informed by common open-source
community standards. It is not a verbatim adoption of an external code of
conduct and therefore requires no external version attribution.
